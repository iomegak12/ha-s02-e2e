01

Clinical Data Usage

1.1 Protected Health Information (PHI): The Platform is designed to process, transmit, and store PHI as defined by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and applicable amendments. Users are strictly prohibited from utilizing the Platform for unauthorized extraction, dissemination, or utilization of PHI outside the explicit scope of direct patient care or authorized clinical administration.

1.2 Anonymization & Aggregation: MedAdmin Pro reserves the right to aggregate and fully anonymize clinical metadata to optimize system performance, develop predictive administrative models, and ensure platform stability. No personally identifiable patient data shall ever be utilized in aggregate telemetry.

Notice on Data Residency:

All clinical data managed through MedAdmin Pro is hosted entirely within redundant, geographically isolated Tier 4 data centers situated within the continental United States, ensuring compliance with strict domestic residency mandates.

02

Privacy Policy

2.1 Administrative Telemetry: We collect non-clinical diagnostic, technical, and usage information about your interaction with the Platform. This includes IP addresses, browser types, interaction timestamps, and audit ledger events. This data is critical for providing the "ProblemDetails" API schema and ensuring zero-trust security architecture.

2.2 Third-Party Integrations: Where the Platform integrates with external Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems (e.g., Epic, Cerner), the flow of data is governed by independent Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). MedAdmin Pro acts solely as a conduit and processor as defined by those specific BAAs.

03

Regulatory Compliance

3.1 Audit Ledgers: All administrative actions (creation, modification, deletion of records) are immutably logged in our core ledger. These logs are accessible via the "Audit Logs" module and are formatted specifically to meet standards required by internal compliance officers and external regulatory audits.

4.1 Credential Hygiene: Users are explicitly prohibited from sharing authentication credentials. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all administrative access levels. Any suspected compromise must be immediately reported through the platform's security incident workflow.

4.2 Device Security: Accessing the Platform via public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks without an approved VPN tunnel is a direct violation of this agreement and may result in immediate, automated account suspension pending review.

  • Ensure endpoints have active, approved EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response) agents.
  • Do not export Platform data to unauthorized portable media.
  • Adhere to automatic session timeout policies (default: 15 minutes of inactivity).
